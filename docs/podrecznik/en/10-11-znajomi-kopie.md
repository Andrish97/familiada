# 10. Friends and managing friendships

## 10.1 Who is a friend in the app

A friend is another person with an account in the app whom you connected with through an invitation: one
person sent it, the other accepted it. Both need a regular account with a confirmed email
address — a guest cannot have friends.

A friend is not the same as a subscriber. A subscriber has no account and only gets
emails from you about polls and shared devices (→ 14.1).

## 10.2 Sending invitations

1. Open the *Friends* page and the "Invitations" tab.
2. In the "Add a friend" section, enter the person's **full** email address or **full** username
   — the app does not suggest names.
3. Choose [Invite].

The invitation appears in the "Pending and declined" section. If the invited person has
notifications turned on, they get an email with an "Open invitation" button.

If there is no account for that address, the app tells you so and offers to add the person
as a subscriber (→ 14.2). It also works the other way round: if you enter the address of a person with an
account on the *Subscribers* page, the app offers to invite them as a friend.

## 10.3 Handling invitations and their statuses

**Received invitations** are in the "Friends" tab, in the "Suggestions" section:

- [Accept] — the person is added to your friends list,
- [Decline] — the invitation moves to the collapsed "Declined by me" section. For
  30 days you can bring it back with the [Undo decline] button and then accept it.

**Sent invitations** are in the "Invitations" tab, in the "Pending and declined" section:

- pending — you can [Resend] them (once the required waiting time has passed) or [Withdraw] them,
- declined — you can only [Withdraw] them.

After an invitation is declined, neither of the two people can invite the other for 30 days.
Invitation emails are sent no more often than every 5 days and at most twice in 30 days.

On a computer, both tabs are shown side by side; on a phone, you switch between them at the top.
The counters next to the tabs show pending invitations.

## 10.4 Managing your friends list

Your friends list is in the "Friends" tab. Each person is shown with their name and email address.

To remove a friend, choose [Remove] and confirm. Then:

- the person disappears from the list and moves to "Declined by me" — for 30 days you can
  undo this with [Undo decline],
- pending poll and question base invitations between the two of you are cancelled,
- **bases and devices you have already shared stay shared** — revoke access to bases (→ 12.5) and devices
  (→ 17.1) separately,
- copies of games and logos you have already sent stay with that person — a sent copy cannot be taken back (→ 11).

## 10.5 What a friendship lets you do

With friends you can:

- invite them to polls (→ 15.2),
- send them copies of games and logos — friends only,
- quickly pick them from a list when sharing bases (→ 12.2) and devices (→ 17.1).

You can also share a base or a device with someone who is not your friend by entering their email address
or username.

# 11. Sending games and logos to friends

## 11.1 What sending a copy means

Your friend gets **their own copy** of the game or logo. They have no access to your original: their
changes will not change your game, and your changes will not change their copy. A sent copy **cannot be taken back**.

How sending differs from other ways of passing things on:

- [Duplicate] makes a copy for you (→ 6.6),
- exporting to a file gives you a file you can pass on to anyone (→ 6.7),
- Community Games is a public catalogue for everyone.

Sending requires an account and at least one friend.

## 11.2 Sending a game

1. In *My games*, select a game and choose [Send].
2. In the "Send to friends" window, select friends.
3. Choose [Send]. The app confirms: "Sent to friends: …".

The copy keeps the game type and all its content. You can send:

- a prepared game — in any state,
- a poll game — only as a **draft**, without poll results; a poll that is open, stopped
  or closed cannot be sent.

You cannot send games added from Community Games, demo materials, or a game that is currently
open in the editor or in settings.

## 11.3 Sending a logo

On the *My logos* page, select a logo, choose [Send] and continue as for a game. Your friend
can use the logo they receive in their own games (→ 8.5). Demo logos cannot be sent.

## 11.4 Receiving copies

When someone sends you a copy, you get an email "… sent you a copy" (if you have notifications
turned on), and a counter appears next to the *My games* or *My logos* tab.

The copy waits in the "Received copies" section — with its name and a "From: …" label:

- [Accept] — the copy goes into your library: a game into the tab for its type, a logo into
  *My logos*. It keeps the "From: …" label, and in *My games* you can also find it with
  the "Received" filter.
- [Decline] — the copy disappears. The same person cannot send it to you again for 30 days.

A copy you do not accept within **5 days** disappears. While it waits for your decision, it cannot
be edited, duplicated or deleted.

## 11.5 Send history

In the "Send to friends" window, expand the "Send history" section. You can see who you
sent copies to, and when, over the last 30 days. The history is for viewing only — it does not show
whether a copy was accepted, and it does not let you take back a sent copy.
