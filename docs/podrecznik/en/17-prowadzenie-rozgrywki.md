# 17. Running the game and connecting devices

The game is run by the **operator** in the *Control panel*. The other devices show the game to the
players and the host. This chapter describes them together — stage by stage: what the operator does
and what happens on the other screens at the same time. Rules of the rounds and the final → 3 and 4.

## 17.1 Interfaces and connecting devices

| Device | Role | Where it usually runs |
|---|---|---|
| *Control panel* | the operator runs the whole game: reveals answers, gives mistakes, moves on | the operator's computer or tablet |
| **Display** | the board for the players and the audience — answers, points, X, logo | TV, projector, second monitor |
| **Host** | the screen for the person reading the questions — the question, all answers with points | tablet or phone |
| **Buzzer** | two buttons, A and B, for the players in the duel | phone held sideways |

**How to open the Control panel.** Select a ready game in *My games* and choose [Play].
The first step is *Devices*.

**How to connect a device.** Each device has its own card in the panel with a 6-digit code:

- **Display** — [Open] opens it in a new window on this computer; drag the window to
  the TV or a second monitor. On another device, open familiada.online → [Connect device]
  → [Connect by code] and enter the code.
- **Host** and **Buzzer** — scan the [QR Code] with a phone or enter the code via
  [Connect device]. [QR on display] shows the code on the Display, so it is easy to
  scan from the room.

[Copy] copies the code, and [Share] sends the device to another person — enter their email
address or username, or choose a friend. A recipient with an account will find the device
in *Connect a device* → "Devices shared with me", and a person without an account — in the email
and on the *My notifications* page (→ 14.5). Sharing devices requires an account.

The dots in the panel's top bar show which devices are connected.

**Without some of the devices:**

- "No host tablet" — you play without a host screen (for example, the host reads
  from the panel),
- "Physical buzzer" — you use your own buttons or raised hands; the operator marks
  which team was first.

The Display is always needed.

**Test mode** shows all devices in one window — for trying out a game on one computer
(it needs a large window). For a real game, use separate devices (→ 19.4).

## 17.2 Preparing and starting the game

1. **Devices.** Connect the devices (→ 17.1). On the "Sound" card, choose where the sound
   should come from: the *Control panel* or the **Display** (for example, the TV speakers). If the sound comes
   from the Display, press [Unlock sound] on it — without this you cannot continue.
   Choose [Next].
2. **Settings summary.** Check the teams, the look with its preview, the sound volume, and the round and
   final questions. [Reshuffle] changes the drawn questions, [Change settings] opens
   the game settings (→ 16). Choose [Done — start the game].
3. **Starting the game.** [Start game] plays the intro. Then choose [Start] to begin the round.

Before you start, put the Display in full screen and do not close it. The browser may mute
the sound after the page is reloaded or the TV goes to sleep — if so, press
[Unlock sound] again.

## 17.3 Running the rounds

**The duel.**

1. After [Start], the host reads the question. On the host screen, the question is
   covered until the duel — it is uncovered with a swipe.
2. The players press their button (A or B). The panel shows who was first:
   [Accept: {team}] or [Retry]. With a physical buzzer, the operator chooses
   team A or B.
3. The operator marks the player's answer in the list or gives an X. The app settles the duel on its own:
   the top-scoring answer wins straight away; otherwise the other team answers
   and the higher answer wins (in a tie — the first one). If both answers are off the board,
   the next pair of players plays the duel.

**The team's turn.**

- The operator clicks an answer and then confirms it — the answer is revealed on the Display
  with a sound, and the points go into the round pool (on the Display, "SUM").
- [X] adds a mistake (counter "1 / 3", "2 / 3", "3 / 3"); an X appears on the Display with a sound.
- [3s] starts a three-second countdown when an answer is taking too long. When the time
  runs out, the app adds an X on its own. Clicking again or doing anything else turns the countdown off without an X.
- [Pass control] hands the question over to the opponents — once per round, before the first revealed
  answer.

Answer, mistake and pass-control actions need two clicks: select, then confirm —
this protects you from mistakes.

The host sees the question and all the answers with points on their screen — the revealed ones are
marked. The answers are under a cover, which the host uncovers and covers again with a swipe.

**Steal.** After three mistakes, the panel moves on to the steal ("Round … — steal").
The opponents give one answer: the operator marks it or gives an X. A hit moves the pool
to the opponents, a miss leaves it with the playing team.

**End of the round.** [End round] awards the pool multiplied by the round multiplier. Then
the operator can reveal the remaining answers and chooses [Go to next round],
[Go to the final] or [Go to end of game] — depending on the threshold, the final settings
and the number of questions (→ 3.6, 3.7).

## 17.4 Running the final

1. [Start] the final. The second finalist should leave and put on headphones (→ 4.1).
2. **First finalist.** [Start countdown (15s)] — the host reads five questions, and the operator
   types the answers into five fields. The countdown can be started only once; it can be stopped after
   all the fields are filled in.
3. **Scoring the answers.** For each question, the operator chooses the matching answer from the list,
   "Not on the list (0 pts)" or "No answer", and then [Show answer] and [Show points]
   — the Display reveals them one by one. [Next] moves on to the next question.
4. [Start] the second round of the final — the second finalist comes back; the first finalist's answers are hidden.
5. **Second finalist.** [Start countdown (20s)]. If the second finalist repeats an answer given by
   the first, the operator presses [Repeat] — a signal sounds, and the finalist gives a different answer.
   Scoring the answers — as in step 3.

The host sees the questions, the finalists' answers and how they were matched. When the finalists reach the target
(200 by default → 4.3), the operator can choose [Finish final] without revealing the remaining questions.

## 17.5 Ending the game

[Finish game] sums up the result, plays the outro and shows on the Display what was chosen
in the settings: the logo, the points or the prize amount (→ 16.6). Then you can choose
[Start over] or [Back to my games].

Always end the game with this button. If you close the panel in the middle, the game stays in progress
and blocks changes to the settings (→ 16.1). The logo on the host screen depends on the
"Host logo" setting (→ 16.3). Problems with devices → 19.5.
