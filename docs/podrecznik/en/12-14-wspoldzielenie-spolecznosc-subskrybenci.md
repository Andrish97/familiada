# 12. Sharing and editing question bases together

## 12.1 How sharing a base works

A shared base is **one and the same for everyone** who has access to it: changes made
by one person are seen by the others. This is different from sending a copy of a game or logo, where
everyone gets their own, independent copy (→ 11).

A base is shared by its owner; this requires an account (→ 5.1).

## 12.2 Inviting people to a base

1. On the *Question bases* page, select your base and choose [Share] in the bottom bar.
2. Enter an email address or username, or select people in the "Friends" list.
3. At the bottom of the window choose the access level: "Can edit" (the default) or "View only",
   and choose [Share] — with several recipients the button shows their number, e.g.
   [Share (3)]. Everyone from this window gets the same access level.

After sending, the window closes. If an invitation cannot be sent (e.g. the person is no longer
your friend), the window stays open and shows a message.

![Share a base](zrzut:bases-share-send)

The invited person does not have to be your friend, but they must have an account. If they have
notifications turned on, they get an email with a link.

## 12.3 Accepting an invitation and opening a base

You will see the invitation on the *Question bases* page, in the "Shared" tab — as a
"Proposed" tile with a "From: …" label. The counter next to the tab shows the number of invitations.

- [Accept] — the base stays in the "Shared" tab; you open it with the [Browse] button.
- [Decline] — the invitation disappears. You can undo the decline in the declined section.

![Base invitations in the “Shared” tab](zrzut:bases-shared-proposed)
![Declined invitations](zrzut:bases-shared-declined-open)

An invitation that is not accepted expires after **5 days**.

## 12.4 Access levels

| Who | Label | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | a tile on the "My bases" tab | everything: edit, share, rename, [Duplicate] and delete the base |
| Edit | a pencil icon on the tile | add, change, move and delete questions, folders and tags; create games from them; export questions from games into this base (→ 6.7) |
| Read | an eye icon on the tile | browse the base and export it to a file |

A person with read access cannot create a game directly from the base. They can export the base
to a file, import it as their own (→ 9.1) and create a game there.

How to work with questions → 9.

## 12.5 Working together and managing access

**Editing together.** When someone is editing a question, folder or tag, that item is
busy for a moment. If you try to change it at the same time, you will see the message "This database item
is currently being edited in another tab or by another user." Wait a moment
and try again. While someone has the base open, the owner cannot rename or delete it.

**Managing access (owner).** The [Shares] window (button in the top bar) shows
pending, accepted and declined invitations. Next to each person you can:

- change the access level with the "Can edit" / "View only" button — the change is saved
  immediately, even before the invitation is accepted,
- send a reminder about a pending invitation — the next one after the countdown on the button,
- revoke access or cancel an invitation with the trash icon.

![Base shares — list of people with the permission next to each](zrzut:bases-share-tools)

A person whose access is revoked loses it immediately — if they have the base open, they will see the message
"Access revoked". You can also leave a base shared with you yourself with the
[Remove from list] button.

Removing someone from your friends does not revoke their access to bases — do that separately (→ 10.4).

# 13. Community Games

## 13.1 What Community Games are

Community Games is a public catalogue of ready-made games, prepared by users
and by Familiada. When you add a game to your library, you get **your own copy to play**
— not shared access to the author's game. This is different from sending a copy to a friend (→ 11).

## 13.2 Browsing and searching for games

On the *Community Games* page:

- "Search games…" — searches titles and descriptions,
- "Language" — All, Polish, English or Ukrainian,
- "Sort" — Recommended, Top rated, Most added, Newest, Title A–Z.

![Community Games](zrzut:marketplace)
![Language filter](zrzut:logos-market-market-filter)

[Load more] shows more games.

A game card shows the language, the title, the author ("by: …") or a gold "Familiada" label for games
prepared by the app, the description, how many times it was added and the average rating (or "No ratings yet"). The
"In your games" label means the game is already in your library.

![No search results](zrzut:logos-market-market-search-empty)

## 13.3 Game details and ratings

Click a card to open the details: the description and all the questions with their answers and points.
You can look through them before adding the game.

A signed-in user can rate a game from 1 to 5 stars ("Rate this game:") and change the rating
later. You cannot rate your own game. For recommendations, the app only takes the average into account
once a game has three ratings.

![Game details with a rating](zrzut:logos-market-market-detail)
![Details without an account — no rating](zrzut:logos-market-market-guest-detail)

## 13.4 Adding a game to your library and playing it

1. In the game details, choose [Add to my games].
2. The game appears in *My games*, in the "Community Games" tab.
3. Select it and choose [Play] (→ 17). You can also change its [Game settings] (→ 16).

Such a game cannot be edited, have its type changed, be duplicated, exported or
sent.

![A game in the Community Games tab](zrzut:games-market)
![Preview of a library game](zrzut:games-market-preview)

To remove a game from your library, choose [Remove from my games] in the details, or the bin icon on
the tile ("Remove from library"). You only remove your own copy — other people who added this game
still have it.

## 13.5 Submitting your own game

1. On the *Community Games* page, open "My submissions" and choose
   [Submit a new game].
2. Choose a game, enter a title (up to 120 characters) and a description (up to 500 characters), and select the game's language.
3. Tick "I understand the snapshot is immutable after submission." and choose
   [Submit to Community Games].

![Submitting a game](zrzut:logos-market-market-submit)
![No games ready to submit](zrzut:logos-market-market-submit-none)

You can submit your own game that is **ready to play** (→ 7.6): a prepared game or
a game with a closed poll. Demo games and games added from Community Games cannot be submitted. A game can
have only one submission at a time.

A submission is **a snapshot of the game at the moment you send it** — later changes to your game will not
change it. A game from a closed poll goes into the catalogue as a game with ready-made answers
and points.

## 13.6 Submission status and withdrawing a game

In "My submissions" you can see the status of each submission:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Awaiting review | The submission is waiting to be checked. |
| Published | The game is in the catalogue. |
| Rejected | The game was not accepted; the reason is shown with the submission ("Reason: …"). A rejected submission disappears after 5 days; you can improve the game and submit it again. |

![My submissions — statuses](zrzut:logos-market-market-my-sent-all)

You can [Withdraw] a published game. It disappears from the catalogue for new people, but those who
have already added it still have it — with the label "ARCHIVED — removed from Community".

![Withdrawing a game](zrzut:logos-market-market-withdraw)

# 14. Subscribers and email notifications

## 14.1 Who is a subscriber?

A subscriber is a person **without an account** in the app whom you add by email address so that
you can invite them to polls. A subscriber:

- gets emails from you about polls and shared devices,
- answers polls and connects devices without signing in,
- manages their emails on the *My notifications* page.

You add people who have an account as friends (→ 10). When a subscriber creates an account, their address
shows "Has an account — go to Friends.", and you get a friend suggestion from them.

## 14.2 Inviting subscribers

1. Open the *Subscribers* page (on a phone — the "Invitations" tab).
2. In the "Add a subscriber" section, enter an email address and choose [Invite].

An email "Subscription invitation from …" with a "View invitation" button
is sent to that address. The recipient accepts or declines the invitation on the page the email
leads to.

![Subscribers](zrzut:subscribers)
![An address with an account — go to Friends](zrzut:people-subscribers-has-account)

You cannot invite an address that belongs to an account (the app will suggest a friendship instead), or an address whose
owner has opted out of all emails. Invitation emails are sent no more often
than every 5 days and at most twice in 30 days; after a decline, you can invite again only after 30 days.

## 14.3 Subscriber list and statuses

- "Pending" section (under "Add a subscriber"): "Pending" — the invitation is waiting for an answer;
  "Declined" — the recipient said no (shown for 5 days). The filter above the list shows only one status.
- "Subscribers" section: people who have accepted the invitation.

On a computer both parts are on one screen; on a phone, you switch between the "Subscribers" and
"Invitations" tabs at the top.

![Subscribers and invitations](zrzut:people-subscribers-half@desktop)
![Invitation status filter](zrzut:subscribers-filter)

For a pending invitation you can [Resend] (the circular arrow icon). The trash icon ends the subscription and deletes
that person's pending invitations to your polls.

![Countdown to resending](zrzut:people-subscribers-resend-cooldown)
![Removing a subscriber](zrzut:people-subscribers-confirm-remove)

## 14.4 Poll notifications

You choose subscribers as poll recipients together with your friends (→ 15.2). Each of them gets
the email "Voting invitation — …" with a link to vote, and you can send
a reminder. In the same way, a subscriber gets an email about a shared device (→ 17.1).

Every email has a "My notifications" link at the bottom and an option to unsubscribe from all
Familiada emails.

## 14.5 The "My notifications" page and managing your own subscriptions

This page is for people who receive the emails, including those without an account.

**How to get there:**

- click "My notifications" at the bottom of any email from Familiada (the link works for
  30 days), or
- open the *Your Familiada emails* page, enter your address and choose [Send link]. You will get
  an email with a one-time link valid for 15 minutes; after clicking it, choose
  [Open my notifications].

![Entering the page — type your address](zrzut:people-mails-entry)
![Link sent](zrzut:people-mails-sent)
![An address with an account — log in](zrzut:people-mails-registered)

If the address belongs to an account, the app asks you to sign in — you then set your notifications
in *Account settings* (→ 5.8).

**What you will find here:**

- **Subscriptions** — who you can receive polls from. You accept pending ones with [Accept] or
  decline them with [Decline]; you leave an active one with [Unsubscribe] — that person will be able to invite
  you again only after 30 days.
- **Tasks** — polls waiting for your vote: [Vote] or [Decline] (→ 15.3), and devices shared
  with you: [Connect device] (→ 17.1).
- **"Notifications are on"** — [Unsubscribe] turns off all emails: it cancels your subscriptions,
  pending polls and shared devices, and nobody will be able to invite you. If you
  turn notifications back on, the old invitations will not come back.

![Subscriptions and tasks](zrzut:mails-tools)
![Opting out of all notifications](zrzut:people-mails-resign-confirm)

Access to the page expires after an hour of inactivity — after that, come back in through the link.
