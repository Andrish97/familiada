# 8. Game logo: creating and using

## 8.1 What a game logo is

A game logo is a caption or picture that you prepare yourself and that appears during
the game — among other places at the start and at the end of the game. It is not the Familiada app logo:
if you do not choose your own, the game will show the default logo.

You create and store logos on the *My logos* page. You can assign a different
logo to each game (→ 8.5).

![My logos](zrzut:logos)
![A selected logo — action buttons](zrzut:logos-market-logos-selected)

The Display has **150 × 70 dots**. Something that looks good in high resolution can become
unreadable once turned into dots. Thick shapes, large letters and high contrast work best —
thin lines and small details usually disappear. Check the result with the [Preview] button.

> Note: logos are created on a computer or tablet. On a phone you can
> browse the list of logos, but the editor is not available.

![The logo editor is unavailable on a phone](zrzut:logos-market-editor-phone@phone)

## 8.2 Creating a text logo

1. On the *My logos* page, click the "New text logo" tile, enter a name for the logo and confirm.
2. Type the caption (for example "FAMILIADA").

The caption is shown in a font like the classic Familiada logo. The "Width: …/30" counter
shows whether the caption fits on the Display. The [Allowed characters] button shows the characters
you can use. A caption that is too wide or contains characters that are not allowed will not be saved —
the app will point out the problem.

![Text logo](zrzut:logo-text)
![Text too wide](zrzut:logos-market-text-too-wide)
![Allowed characters](zrzut:logos-market-text-chars)

If you want the caption in a different typeface or with extras, use a drawn logo (→ 8.3).

## 8.3 Drawing a logo

Click the "New drawn logo" tile. You draw on the canvas in two colors — black
and white; the white elements will light up on the Display.

| Tool | Shortcut | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Pointer | V | selecting and moving elements |
| Hand | H (or hold the space bar) | moving the view |
| Brush | B | freehand drawing |
| Eraser | E | removes the elements it touches |
| Shapes | S | line, rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star, arrows, heart and more |
| Text | T | captions — with many typefaces to choose from |

![Drawing editor](zrzut:logo-draw)
![Brush](zrzut:logos-market-draw-brush)
![Shapes](zrzut:logos-market-draw-shapes)
![Text — choosing a typeface](zrzut:logos-market-draw-text)
![A selected element — settings](zrzut:logos-market-draw-selected)

You also have: zooming the view in and out, the canvas background (black or white),
line thickness and style, fill, [Undo], [Redo], [Duplicate], [Clear] and [Preview]
— which shows how the logo will look on the Display.

![Scene background](zrzut:logos-market-draw-bg)
![Preview of the drawing on the Display](zrzut:logos-market-draw-preview)

Useful shortcuts and rules:

- Delete (on a Mac Fn+Backspace) or × in the options bar removes the selected elements; [Clear] removes
  the whole drawing after you confirm.
- Ctrl+Z (⌘Z) undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes, Ctrl+D duplicates; Ctrl+0 resets
  the zoom to 100%, and Ctrl + plus and Ctrl + minus zoom the view in and out.
- L — line, R — rectangle, O — ellipse, P — polygon, U — next shape, F — fill;
  [ and ] change the thickness of the line or eraser.
- The arrow keys move the selection by 1 dot, or by 10 with Shift. Holding Ctrl temporarily switches
  to the Pointer.
- Holding Shift draws a square or a circle, and a line or arrow snaps to 15° steps.
- Press Enter to finish a polygon, Esc to cancel it, and Backspace to remove the last point.
- [Undo] and [Redo] work within the current editing session — when you reopen the logo, the history starts
  from scratch.

Keyboard shortcuts do not work while you are typing text.

## 8.4 Creating a logo from an image

1. Click the "New logo from image" tile and choose [Choose image] — a JPG, PNG, GIF
   or WEBP file, up to 5 MB.
2. Set the crop: the frame on the "Display area" card shows the part of the image that will appear on the screen.
   You can rotate the image by 90° and straighten it.
3. Adjust the look with the sliders: brightness, contrast, gamma, black, white and dither (the graininess
   of tonal transitions). [Invert] swaps light and dark areas, [Reset] undoes the settings.

![Image logo — choosing an image](zrzut:logos-market-image-empty)
![Crop and sliders](zrzut:logo-image)
![Preview of the image logo](zrzut:logos-market-image-preview)

The image will be turned into Display dots. On the host screen it can appear
in its original form (→ 8.5).

## 8.5 Saving, choosing and displaying a logo

**Saving.** The logo saves itself while you work — there is no "Save" button. Changes are
also saved when you leave with the [Back] button.

**The "My logos" list.** For the selected logo, [Edit], [Preview], [Duplicate],
export to a `.famlogo` file, [Import] and [Send] — a copy for a friend (→ 11.3) — are available.
You rename a logo by double-clicking it (on a phone, tap twice), and delete it with the trash icon.
Don't edit the contents of `.famlogo` files by hand — importing such a file may fail.

![Logo preview](zrzut:logos-market-logos-preview)
![Renaming](zrzut:logos-market-logos-rename)
![Deleting a logo](zrzut:logos-market-logos-delete)
![Importing a logo from a file](zrzut:logos-market-logos-import-file)

**Choosing a logo in a game.** You choose the logo in *Game settings*, in the "Logo" section — "Default"
or one of yours (→ 16.3).

![Choosing the logo and “Host logo” in the game settings](zrzut:game-settings-display)

**How the logo looks during a game:**

- **The Display** always shows the logo made of dots, because it is a dot screen.
- **The host screen** — depending on the "Host logo" setting (→ 16.3):
  - "Pixels" — the same as the Display,
  - "Source" — a text logo as a caption in the style of the Familiada logo, a logo from an image as the original
    image, a drawn logo as a smooth drawing in the dot color.

The logo appears at the start of the game, and at the end if you set the end screen that way (→ 16.6, 17.5).

![The logo on the Display](zrzut:play-display-logo)

**Locks.** Editing a logo and playing a game exclude each other: while you are editing a logo, you cannot open
the control panel or game settings, and during a game or while game settings are open
you cannot edit or delete a logo. The app will show the reason — finish one activity and go back
to the other. A waiting page loads by itself once the other activity ends (→ 19.2).
